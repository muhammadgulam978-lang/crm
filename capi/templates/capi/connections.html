Why two platforms?

Each platform has its own access token, dataset, and webhook. Configuring them separately keeps permissions scoped: Meta tokens never touch LinkedIn data and vice versa. Conversion events from your CRM are only sent after an admin explicitly enables the event mapping in Event Configuration.
